# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients evaluating Abhilash Khadanga as a full-stack developer. They arrive from a resume link, LinkedIn, or GitHub, spend a few minutes scanning, and are deciding whether to reach out. Desktop-first but must hold up on mobile.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio that proves technical and design craft through the site itself, not just through the projects it lists. Success is a visitor forming a strong impression of care and competence, then acting on it, via email, resume download, or the contact form.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, minimal. Restrained monochrome palette with a single accent color that shifts per chapter (Arrival / Formation / Proof / Invitation). Editorial typography (oversized display headlines, small mono-uppercase labels), generous negative space, and purposeful GSAP-driven scroll motion rather than decorative animation. Confidence is shown through restraint, not loudness.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS marketing-site patterns: hero-metric blocks, identical icon-card grids, gradient-text headlines, glassmorphism panels.
- Cluttered "look how much I can build" portfolios that undercut the precise/confident tone.
- Boxy, form-in-a-card contact sections that feel like a support ticket form rather than part of the editorial page.

## Design Principles

- Restraint reads as confidence: one accent color, generous whitespace, no decoration that doesn't carry meaning.
- Typography and motion ARE the design system; avoid leaning on cards/boxes/shadows as a crutch.
- Every section should feel like a continuation of the same authored voice, not a bolted-on widget.
- Motion should clarify structure (reveals, transitions between chapters) never just decorate.
- Mobile is a first-class layout, not a squeezed-down desktop view.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA contrast. Site already respects `prefers-reduced-motion` throughout (instant/no-op fallbacks for every GSAP sequence) — carry this into any new work. Keyboard-focusable interactive elements (links, form fields, buttons) with visible focus states.
